Nano

A personal assistant app that runs on one person's own computer.

Nano is a private, single-user app built by and for Avi Cagan. It runs on his own Windows PC and helps him with everyday things: answering his messages, keeping track of tasks, and recognizing who is texting him.

Nano is not a public product. It isn't offered to anyone else, and nobody but its owner can sign in to it.

Google account access

With its owner's permission, Nano reads his Google Contacts (read-only) so it can tell him who a message is from. It never changes or deletes contacts. See the privacy policy for exactly what it reads and where that stays.
