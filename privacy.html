Privacy policy

Nano · last updated November 20, 2026

Nano is a personal app that runs on its owner's own computer. It has one user, its owner, and it isn't offered to the public. This page explains what Nano does with data from his Google account.

What Nano reads

  • Google Contacts, read-only (the contacts.readonly permission): each contact's name, nickname, phone numbers and email addresses.

Why

Only to recognize who is messaging the owner, and to find a contact when he asks for one. Nano never changes, adds or deletes contacts.

Where it stays

  • A copy of the contact list is kept on the owner's own computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection so only his Windows account can read it. It's refreshed every few hours.
  • The sign-in permission Google issues is stored the same way, on the same computer.
  • Contact data is not sent to any server run by Nano (there isn't one), and it is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Nano's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Nano's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Nano's data folder on his computer removes the stored copy.
